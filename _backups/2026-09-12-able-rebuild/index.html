Client & Challenge
Able Australia is a registered NDIS provider and not-for-profit supporting people with disability and older people across Victoria, Tasmania, Queensland and the ACT. The brief was to make a broad service offer feel clearer, more human and easier to recognise across audiences and locations.
Design Scope
Brand strategy · Visual identity · Logo system · Campaign art direction · Social, out-of-home and staff touchpoints
Design Approach
The identity pairs a responsive wing-shaped ‘A’ with high-contrast blue and yellow, direct language and person-centred photography. Repetition, scale and modular layouts create recognition across formats; the result is a flexible system that keeps people—not services—at the centre.
A More Human Identity
Bold type and an animated sequence move from organisation to collective voice—Able, Together, We are Able. The system turns the brand name into an active statement of belonging.

Campaign Language
Short, affirmative messages translate the brand promise into everyday actions: do more, make, move, connect. Consistent type, generous scale and image-led layouts let each message adapt without losing the wider system.
Out-of-Home Presence
Large-format posters pair direct language with person-centred photography. The repeated identity mark and clear hierarchy are designed for quick recognition at street level, while the human image keeps the message grounded in real life.
Brand Touchpoints
Bottles, totes and staff credentials carry the same visual system into daily use. Rather than treating merchandise as decoration, each object reinforces recognition and helps the identity feel present, practical and connected.
