Project BackgroundGeGe is a local pancake shop whose name suggests the feeling of satisfaction after eating. The redesign develops this playful idea into a more distinctive identity while retaining the shop’s familiar pink colour palette.
Design ScopeLogo Redesign · Custom Lettering · Character Illustration · Packaging · Brand Applications
Design ApproachRounded and inflated letterforms reference the soft texture of pancakes, while melting and bitten details introduce associations with butter, sweetness and appetite. The lettering, colour palette and character illustration are extended across different touchpoints to create a friendly and cohesive visual language.
VISUAL · IDENTITY GeGe Pancake Shop ← ALL WORK
Takeaway System Cups, cards and stickers operate as one coordinated family. The primary cup forms establish the character-led identity; supporting print pieces extend it through ordering, gifting and takeaway moments.
