Instagram Reel The reel moves between lifestyle detail and product-led frames, using consistent pacing and typography to turn the campaign into a concise social narrative. Instagram Story Vertical stories use room-scale photography, restrained typography and a single product message, keeping the call to action legible within a fast-moving social format.
Project BackgroundDomayne is an Australian retailer specialising in contemporary furniture, bedding, homewares and electrical products. This project presents a selection of seasonal and product-led campaigns created across the brand’s digital channels.
Design ScopeLanding Pages · Web Tiles · EDMs · Motion Graphics · Social Reels
Design ApproachThe visual approach balances aspirational lifestyle imagery with the clarity required for retail communication. Modular layouts establish a clear relationship between campaign messaging, product categories and calls to action, allowing each concept to be adapted across different formats while maintaining a consistent brand experience.
