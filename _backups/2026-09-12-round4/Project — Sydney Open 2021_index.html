Project Background
Sydney Open is an annual event celebrating Sydney’s architecture, heritage and built environment. The 2021 program, Reimagine Your City, combined online and outdoor experiences including talks, audio tours, podcasts and virtual building tours.
Design Scope
Poster Series · Tri-fold Brochure · Web Banners · Animated GIF Advertising
Design Approach
Architectural silhouettes, map-inspired forms and line drawings connect the visual identity to the structure of the city. A vivid pink and pale mint palette gives the campaign a contemporary and highly visible character. The modular system adapts across posters, editorial information and digital advertising while maintaining a consistent event identity.
Posters
Three posters share one system: a flat silhouette of a Sydney landmark, a cream-and-magenta field, and a fixed type stack. The headline poster carries the dates; the two sub-event posters swap the landmark and the message while holding the same frame.
Context
Sydney Open unlocks buildings that are normally closed — halls, archives, tunnels and clock towers across the city. The identity had to sit against that architecture without competing with it, which is why each landmark is reduced to a single flat silhouette.
Print
Produced at A3 and A4 so the same artwork runs as a street poster and as a take-home handout. The two-colour build keeps the silhouette legible at both sizes.
In Situ
Pasted at street scale, the poster is read at a glance and on an angle. The silhouette does the work at distance; the dates and program detail only need to hold up close.
Brochure
An A3 fold-out designed to stay readable at every stage of opening, including fully unfolded. The reverse carries a check box for each sub-event, so the brochure doubles as the visitor’s route plan.
Venues
Nine sites opened across the weekend, from the Fullerton at the GPO Building to Mortuary Station and the St James tunnels. The brochure lists them as a checklist so visitors can plan a route and mark off what they have seen.
