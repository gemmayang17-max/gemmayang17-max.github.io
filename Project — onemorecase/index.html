Project BackgroundI started onemorecase in 2024 with a simple thought: to bring the illustrations I was making into the small objects people use every day. One idea led to another, then to products, packaging, photographs and a whole visual world of its own. I built the brand independently, step by step. Since then, it has grown to over 5,000 subscribers, more than 8,000 products sold online and through retail partners, and consistent five star customer ratings.
