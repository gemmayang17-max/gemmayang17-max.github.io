Project BackgroundThis self-initiated project explores how one visual concept can develop from experimental letterforms into a complete editorial system. It includes a custom display typeface and a three-volume encyclopaedia series exploring flowers, fungi and shells.
Design ScopeCustom Typeface · Book-cover System · Editorial Layout · Hand-drawn Illustration
Design ApproachThe structure and directional forms of an anchor are translated into a consistent letterform language with a subtle metallic and vintage character. Applying the typeface across the covers and internal layouts tests how an expressive typographic idea can remain distinctive while functioning within a controlled grid and readable editorial structure.
