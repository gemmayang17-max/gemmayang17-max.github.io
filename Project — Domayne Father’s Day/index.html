The landing page opens with the campaign image and date-led message, then moves into a clear product hierarchy so visitors can shift from inspiration to shopping without competing visual cues.
Project BackgroundA seasonal Father’s Day campaign for Domayne, developed to connect gift inspiration with clear retail pathways across landing pages, display advertising and email.
Design ScopeLanding Page · Animated Display Advertising · EDM Campaign
Design ApproachEditorial lifestyle photography is paired with a restrained blue-green palette, date-led messaging and modular product blocks. The system keeps the emotional campaign image prominent while adapting cleanly to wide, square and long-form retail formats.
