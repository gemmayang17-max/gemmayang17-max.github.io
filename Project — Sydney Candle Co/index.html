Scent Your Space
 Turn Every Room into Summer
Project BackgroundThis seasonal packaging concept develops a Summer collection for Sydney Candle Co, presented in the project brief as an Australian coastal lifestyle brand. The collection includes three reed diffuser variants, each representing a different summer mood.
Design ScopePackaging Design · Botanical Illustration · Surface Pattern · Colour System
Design ApproachBotanical patterns and colour-coded variants translate the character of each fragrance into a distinct visual atmosphere. Blue, yellow and pink create clear differentiation, while a repeated label structure and consistent information hierarchy connect the products as one collection. The system balances individual product personality with overall brand recognition.
