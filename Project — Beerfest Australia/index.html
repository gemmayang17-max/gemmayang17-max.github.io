Project Background
BeerFest Australia is a nationwide festival celebrating craft beer, cider and cocktails alongside street food, music and live entertainment. The website redesign is centred on Local Community Spirit, reflecting the festival’s emphasis on shared experiences and social connection.
Design Scope
Six-page Website · Responsive Design · Information Architecture · Interaction System
Design Approach
Warm colours, expressive typography and candid festival photography capture the energy of the live event. Clear content hierarchy and repeated ticket pathways guide visitors from discovering the festival to finding practical information and taking action. Responsive layouts and consistent interaction states extend the same experience across desktop and mobile.
