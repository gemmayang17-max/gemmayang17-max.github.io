The identity reduces the project idea to one repeatable mark. Its half-arch and paired blocks scale from app icon to wordmark and public-facing pattern without losing recognition. Color Your Life Color Your Life Colour U Identity Colour U
Project BackgroundColour U is an urban greening app concept that converts everyday walking into visible environmental participation. Daily steps become virtual plants that can contribute to real-world community greenery.
Design ScopeMobile Interface · App Icon · Visual Identity · Illustration · Campaign Poster
Design ApproachA half-arch and paired blocks form a compact symbol that suggests both a planted canopy and two footsteps. Repeating this geometry across the icon, campaign pattern and interface creates a recognisable system, while soft green and pink distinguish environmental progress from user actions.
MOBILE · UI DESIGN Colour U ← ALL WORK
Poster Repeating the identity mark at civic scale turns a small app symbol into an immediately recognisable public graphic, linking individual participation with the shared urban environment. Mobile App The interface carries the same arch motif through onboarding, step conversion, plant selection, redemption and community sharing. Consistent spacing and colour make the multi-step journey easy to scan.
